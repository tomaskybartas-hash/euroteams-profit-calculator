Controller: UAB Euroteams Group, Lithuania, company code 302411430. Contact: tomas@euroteams.eu.

Your application

We collect the contact details, residence country, profession, experience, certificates, languages, availability and work preferences you choose to provide. We process your application to consider you for recruitment at your request. Optional permission for future job offers is recorded separately and may be withdrawn without affecting your current application.

Review and matching

Your information is stored in Euroteams' recruitment system hosted on Render and compared with project requirements. Matching supports recruiter review; it does not make a final employment decision. Self-declared qualifications and availability are not independently verified by submitting this form. Your profile is accessible to authorized Euroteams staff. Further sharing with a client requires a separate recruitment step.

Retention and your choices

We retain applications for up to 24 months after the last recruitment interaction, unless a shorter period is requested or another justified obligation applies. Contact us to request access, correction, export, deletion, restriction or withdrawal of job-offer permission. Retention review and these requests are currently handled by Euroteams staff, rather than automatically.

A form-security cookie lasts up to one hour. A keyed, non-public network identifier limits abusive submissions; no advertising cookies are used by this registration form. You may complain to the Lithuanian State Data Protection Inspectorate.
